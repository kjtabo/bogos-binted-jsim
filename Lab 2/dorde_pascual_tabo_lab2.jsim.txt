* ============================= PAGE ONE =============================
* IMPORTANT: the 'n' prefix in variables are used to denote an inverted state
* IMPORTANT: the '_' denotes OR in the fx subckt only

* Inverting the outputs of NAND and NOR gates used less gates compared to inverting the inputs and then using the complementary gates instead
* This uses a total of 8 gates
.subckt fx a b c d e f g out
    Xnabc a b c nabc nand3
    Xabc nabc abc inverter
    Xnde d e nde nand2
    Xde nde de inverter
    Xnfg f g nfg nand2
    Xfg nfg fg inverter
    Xnabc_de_fg abc de fg nabc_de_fg nor3
    Xabc_de_fg nabc_de_fg out inverter
.ends

* Similarly, the same approach was used for the full adder. However, for readability, the half adders used in the full adder were made into its own subckt

* The half adder subckt to be used for the full adder
.subckt ha a b sum carry
    Xsum a b sum xor2
    Xncarry a b nab nand2
    xcarry nab carry inverter
.ends

* This uses a total of 8 gates
.subckt fa a b carry_in sum carry_out
    Xha_one a b ab_sum carry_one ha
    Xha_two ab_sum carry_in sum carry_two ha
    Xncarry carry_one carry_two ncarry nor2
    Xcarry ncarry carry_out inverter
.ends
